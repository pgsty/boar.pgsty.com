The kernel separates *what exists*, *what can be done*, *what is intended*, and
*what actually happened*.

These contracts remain authoritative across the product-first trains. The
v0.7 journeys have passed local real-target acceptance but have not been
officially released; v0.8 source is still under development as of 2026-09-08.

- [Resources and identity](resources/) — the managed object and the evidence that identifies it.
- [Capabilities](capabilities/) — intrinsic support, effective authority, and explicit reasons.
- [Plans and policy](plans/) — immutable intent, plan hashes, preconditions, and admission.
- [Operations and events](operations/) — durable execution, leases, locks, and recovery.
- [Risk and approval](risk/) — the kernel risk scale, approval freshness, and BreakGlass.
- [Drivers](drivers/) — typed provider boundaries under one northbound model.
