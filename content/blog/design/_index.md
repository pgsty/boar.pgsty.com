These 15 notes explain Boar's design decisions. They summarize repository
sources; they do not create new contracts or prove that a feature has shipped.
Their original publication dates are retained, while `lastmod` records later
corrections. Each note distinguishes the design from its current implementation
where that distinction matters and lists its source files.

For current scope, the authority is the Boar repository's
`_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/control-plane-roadmap-v0.7-v1.0.md`, together
with the Charter's product definition and CKRs and accepted ADR amendments.
The numbered PRD, original architecture, and historical receipts explain prior
trains and preserve their compatibility obligations.

Read [Status and roadmap](/docs/develop/roadmap/) for this site's summary of
accepted journeys, work in progress, and publication limits. A typed contract,
a prototype, a locally accepted journey, and an official release are separate
claims.
